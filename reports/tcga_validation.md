# TCGA validation: tumor purity from frozen lung slides

Generated by `python -m project.tcga_report` from `results/tcga_validation_v3.json`. Don't edit by hand.

**Question.** Does the app's whole-slide tumor % track ABSOLUTE purity better than the pathologist's %?

**Data.** TCGA-LUAD TS/BS (frozen) slides; truth = ABSOLUTE purity from DNA. Test split: **150 slides from 15 hospitals** never used for training or for choosing the method; 113 have truth below 60%. Model: conch, organ prompts: lung, prompt set v3.

## Results on the test hospitals

| Method | MAE (points) | Pearson r | Within-hospital r | Catch rate < 60% |

| Pathologist, bias-corrected (fit on train) | 13.1 | 0.23 | 0.21 | 1.00 |
| Always guess the train median | 13.9 | – | – | 1.00 |
| Pathologist (recorded in TCGA) | 34.1 | 0.23 | 0.21 | 0.11 |
| App zero-shot tumor % | 18.4 | 0.32 | 0.26 | 0.65 |
| App zero-shot tumor %, mapped (fit on train) | 13.6 | -0.32 | -0.26 | 1.00 |

## Is the difference real? (1,000 paired bootstrap resamples of the test slides, 95% interval)

| Comparison | Difference (interval) | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Ranking: method r − pathologist r | 0.25 (0.09 to 0.43) | better |
| MAE: bias-corrected pathologist − method | 1.48 (0.36 to 2.62) | better |
| MAE: always-guess-median − method | 2.31 (1.18 to 3.51) | better |
| MAE: recorded pathologist − method | 22.54 (19.15 to 25.73) | better |

"better" means the whole interval is above 0. The recorded pathologist estimates run high, so beating them on MAE is a low bar (the always-guess-median baseline does too); the bias-corrected pathologist, the dummy and the ranking comparison are the meaningful tests.

## Limits

- One cancer type (lung adenocarcinoma) and frozen sections only; the app's main use is FFPE.
- TCGA's pathologist % was largely a quality-control gate for sequencing, not a careful clinical estimate.
- The method is a small model trained on frozen CONCH features, not the app's zero-shot prompts; the app does not use it yet.
- Independent recheck: `scripts/independent_check.py`, written without the project's metric code, recomputed every number above from the per-slide predictions and matched.
